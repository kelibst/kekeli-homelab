## 📂 **Most Useful Features**

### 1. Auto-Backup Photos from Phone
**Setup once, never worry about losing photos!**

1. Open Nextcloud app on phone
2. Menu → Settings → Auto upload
3. Turn on "Enable auto upload"
4. Choose which folders to upload (Camera, Screenshots, etc.)
5. Photos automatically backup when charging & on WiFi!

**Result**: All your family photos safe in one place!

---

### 2. Share Files with Family
**Share vacation photos, documents, anything!**

1. In Files, find the file/folder
2. Click the share icon (person+)
3. Enter family member's name
4. Set permissions (can edit, can view only)
5. Click Share

**Or create a link**:
1. Click share icon
2. "+ Create share link"
3. Copy link and send via WhatsApp/text
4. Set expiration date if needed

---

### 3. Shared Family Calendar
**Everyone sees the same schedule!**

1. Open Calendar app

## 🆘 **Common Questions**

### "I forgot my password"
- Admin can reset: Users → Click user → Enter new password

### "Photos not appearing"
- Wait a few minutes for scan to complete
- Or: Settings → Administration → Files → Scan files

### "Can't access from outside home"
- This is local network only (for security)
- Only accessible when connected to your WiFi/network

### "App not showing up"
- Click grid icon (top right) → See all apps
- Apps are there, just need to click!

### "How much space do I have?"
- Check bottom left of Files app
- Shows used/total space
